Wholesale line sheets, made from your Shopify products

A buyer asks for your line sheet, and the one you have still shows last season's prices. We are planning a small Shopify app that builds the PDF from your live products, so it is current every time you send it.

What we plan to build

  • Built from your storeProducts, variants, SKUs and photos come from Shopify. You add wholesale prices, minimums and terms once.
  • Looks like your brandYour logo, your fonts, a cover page and a terms page, in a layout buyers are used to reading.
  • Current when you send itChange a price or retire a product, and the next PDF reflects it. No rebuilding by hand.
