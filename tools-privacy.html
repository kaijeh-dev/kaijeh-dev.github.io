Privacy for tool sign-ups

Effective 19 November 2026, updated the same day. This covers the sign-up pages for the planned line-sheet and certificate tools.

Who we are

Kaijeh is operated by Aashima Taneja, Mumbai, India. Contact: hello@kaijeh.com.

What we collect

Your email address, your answer to the optional question if you give one, and a short tag saying which page or forum link brought you here. The pages set no cookies and use no tracking scripts.

How it reaches us

The sign-up form is handled by Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA), which receives what you enter and forwards it to our mailbox. Formspree keeps a copy of each submission for a limited time under its own privacy policy. Our mailbox is hosted by Google Workspace.

What we use it for

To email you once when the tool is ready to try, and to understand whether and how to build it. We do not sell or share it, and we do not add you to any other mailing list.

How long we keep it

Until we have sent that one email, or until 30 June 2027 if the tool is not built, whichever comes first. Then we delete it.

Your choices

Email hello@kaijeh.com at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
